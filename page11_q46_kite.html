Proof summary:

From the congruence we proved, is it true that AB = AD?

In congruent triangles, corresponding sides are equal.
From the congruence ΔABC ≅ ΔADC, does AB correspond to AD?

Correct! AB = AD because they are corresponding sides in congruent triangles!
From the congruence ΔABC ≅ ΔADC:
The correspondence is: A↔A, B↔D, C↔C
Therefore the corresponding sides are:
• AB = AD (first sides)
• BC = DC (second sides)
• AC = AC (third sides - shared)

Conclusion: AB = AD
